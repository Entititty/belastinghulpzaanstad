Tax help in Zaandijk

Zaandijk is a picturesque ribbon village on the western bank of the Zaan with around 8,500 residents. The village lies next to the Zaanse Schans and is known for its monumental wooden houses and characteristic Zaanse architecture along the water. Zaandijk has a strong sense of community and many long-term residents who have lived here for generations.

Due to the historic character of many homes in Zaandijk, there are specific tax considerations. Residents of listed buildings are, under certain conditions, entitled to deduct maintenance costs — something we know well. At the same time, first-time buyers buying their first home here can get back up to €280 per month via the mortgage interest deduction.

We know Zaandijk and help you at home — at your kitchen table, without rush, with attention to your personal situation.

Questions about tax return in Zaandijk

Yes, owners of national monuments may, under certain conditions, deduct maintenance costs from their income tax. Zaandijk has a relatively large number of listed buildings. We look at your situation and check whether your home qualifies and which costs are deductible.

A preliminary tax refund is a monthly payment from the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) based on your expected tax benefit. For a Zaandijk home of €330,000 you receive around €280 per month, directly into your account.

Think of: your DigiD, annual statement(s), WOZ valuation (property tax assessment) from municipality of Zaanstad, annual mortgage statement, bank statements (box 3) and possibly healthcare costs, donations or monument maintenance costs. After you sign up we send you a personal checklist.

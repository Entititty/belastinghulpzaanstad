Tax return for seniors 2026 — complete guide for AOW recipients

📅 Updated: April 2026 ⏱ Reading time: 7 minutes ✍ Belastinghulp Zaanstad

Many seniors think they don't have to file a tax return because they only receive AOW or pension. That's a costly misconception. Retirees get back on average €400 to €1,200 via tax credits, healthcare costs deduction and other items. In this guide you'll read what you have to declare, which benefits you deserve — and you can have your situation checked for free via WhatsApp.

When do you as a senior have to file a tax return?

You are required to file a tax return if the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) sends you a tax return letter. But filing a tax return without a letter can also be beneficial. The Belastingdienst sends you a letter if they expect that you have to pay extra tax or get a refund.

Typical situations in which a tax return for seniors pays off:

  • You receive a supplementary pension or annuity in addition to AOW
  • You have healthcare costs that exceed the threshold
  • You have savings or investments (box 3)
  • You have made donations to a charity (ANBI)
  • The elderly tax credit has not been (fully) settled in your payroll tax

Did you know? Many seniors in Zaanstad receive an annual refund of €200 to €800 purely by filing a tax return — money that otherwise stays with the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority).

Deductions especially for seniors

These are the deductions that seniors are most often entitled to but know least about:

  • Healthcare costs deduction — personal contributions WLZ/WMO, dentist, glasses/lenses, diet costs, aids. Deductible above an income-dependent threshold.
  • Donations deduction — periodic donations to a charity (ANBI, e.g. church, museum, aid organisation) are fully deductible.
  • Travel costs for hospital visits — visits to a specialist or hospital are sometimes deductible as part of healthcare costs.
  • Imputed rental value (eigenwoningforfait) minus mortgage interest deduction — do you still have a (small) mortgage? Then this can work out favourably.

What do you need for the tax return?

  • DigiD (or ask someone to log in for you)
  • Annual statement(s) from SVB, pension fund, employer
  • WOZ valuation (property tax assessment) from the municipality
  • Bank statement or overview as of 1 January (for box 3)
  • Receipts for healthcare costs (dentist, personal contributions, aids)
  • Possibly: donation receipts from charities (ANBI)

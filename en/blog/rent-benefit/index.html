⚡ Quick answer

Rent benefit (huurtoeslag) can be up to €522 per month in 2026 — over €6,200 a year. Your entitlement depends on income, rent (maximum eligible rent €932.93) and assets. We check for free whether you qualify.

What is rent benefit?

Rent benefit (huurtoeslag, also called rent subsidy) is a monthly contribution from the government for renters with low or middle income who rent a home in the private or social sector. The aim is to keep housing affordable. The benefit is calculated based on your income, the number of residents, your rent and your wealth.

You apply for rent benefit via the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) Benefits — but you can also ask for help when applying. We are happy to help residents of Zaanstad with this.

Note: You apply for rent benefit via toeslagen.nl or the DigiD app. Don't have DigiD or trouble with digital applications? We help you with it — also at your kitchen table at home.

Conditions for rent benefit in 2026

To qualify for rent benefit you have to meet four main conditions:

  • You rent an independent home (with your own front door, kitchen and toilet)
  • Your rent is under the maximum rent limit (liberalisation threshold)
  • Your income is below the income limit
  • Your wealth is below the assets limit

In addition: you are 18 or older, you have Dutch nationality or a valid residence permit, and the home is your main residence.

Rent limit 2026: maximum rent

To receive rent benefit, your basic rent (excl. service costs) must not exceed the liberalisation threshold. In 2026 the maximum rent limit is:

Age of renter Maximum basic rent 2026
Younger than 23 years ± €454.47 per month
23 years or older ± €808.06 per month

* Exact limits are set annually. Check the current amounts at toeslagen.nl.

Do you have co-residents? The rent limit applies to the entire rental price of the home, not per person. Service costs (gas, water, electricity, cleaning) usually don't count towards the rent limit — but check this carefully on your rental contract.

Income limits rent benefit 2026

Your assessable income (toetsingsinkomen) determines whether you are entitled to rent benefit and how much. This is your taxable income from box 1, 2 and 3. The 2026 income limits are:

Household composition Maximum assessable income (toetsingsinkomen)
Single person (younger than AOW age) ± €31,750
Single person (AOW age or older) ± €34,150
Multi-person household (younger than AOW) ± €43,150
Multi-person household (AOW age or older) ± €46,550

* Indicative amounts for 2026. The Belastingdienst adjusts these limits annually.

Tip: Do you have a partner or co-resident aged 18 or older? Then their income also counts towards the assessable income. Young people up to 23 living with their parents fall outside this calculation.

Assets test: your savings also count

In addition to your income, the Belastingdienst (Dutch Tax Authority) also looks at your wealth (savings, investments, second home). If your wealth exceeds the exemption, you are not entitled to rent benefit. The asset limits for 2026 are:

Situation Maximum wealth
Single person ± €36,952
With a benefit partner (per person) ± €36,952 (together ± €73,904)

* The assets limit equals the tax-free allowance in box 3. Check the current amounts each year.

How much rent benefit can you get?

The amount of your rent benefit depends on your rent, your income and your household composition. The government uses a so-called norm rent: the part of the rent you have to pay yourself. The benefit covers the difference between your actual rent and the norm rent.

Example calculation Amount per month
Bare rent €680
Assessable income (toetsingsinkomen) €22,000 (single)
Standard rent (own contribution) ± €230
Rent benefit per month ± €450

* Indicative example calculation. Your actual benefit depends on your specific income, rent amount and household composition. Calculate your benefit via toeslagen.nl or get in touch with us.

Applying for rent benefit: step by step

You apply for rent benefit via the website of the Belastingdienst (Dutch Tax Authority) Benefits department. These are the steps:

  1. Log in at toeslagen.nl with your DigiD
  2. Choose 'Apply for rent benefit' in the menu
  3. Enter your details: rent, address and income (an estimate for the current year)
  4. Add co-residents who are 18 years or older
  5. Submit the application — you receive a decision and possibly an advance payment

⚠️ Apply on time: You can apply for rent benefit up to 1 September of the year following the year you want the benefit for. So don't wait too long — every month you're late, you miss out on benefit. You can also apply retroactively, up to that same deadline.

Reporting changes: important all year round

Has your situation changed? Report it as soon as possible via Mijn Toeslagen (the online benefits portal). This applies to:

  • A change in income (higher or lower)
  • Moving house or a new rent
  • Moving in together or separating
  • A co-resident moving in or out

If you have received too much benefit, you will have to pay it back. Reporting changes on time prevents an unpleasant reclaim afterwards.

What if you received too much rent benefit?

After the end of the year, the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) settles based on your actual income (the definitive assessment). If your actual income was higher than estimated, you have to pay back (part of) the benefit received. This can be thousands of euros if you don't adjust it in time.

Can't pay the reclaim all at once? Then you can request a payment plan from the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) Benefits — arrange this as early as possible.

Tax return 2025 deadline. 1 May 2026

📅 Last updated: 17 April 2026 ⏱ Reading time: 6 minutes ✍ Stan van der Hoeven

The deadline for the tax return over 2025 is 1 May 2026. That means you only have a few weeks left to submit your tax return. Haven't got round to it yet? No panic – in this guide you'll read exactly what the deadline means, what happens if you file late, and how we can arrange your tax return quickly and correctly for you. Fixed price €59.

⚡ Quick answer

The deadline for the 2025 tax return is 1 May 2026. File before 1 April and the Tax Authority guarantees a response before 1 July, so you receive any refund sooner.

When is the deadline for tax return 2025?

The official deadline for the income tax return for tax year 2025 is Thursday 1 May 2026. This is the latest date by which your tax return must be received by the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority), unless you have applied for an extension.

SituationDeadline
Standard personal tax return1 May 2026
Extension applied for (yourself)1 September 2026
Tax return from abroad (C-form)1 July 2026
Tax return for deceased (F-form)8 months after death

Note: Have you submitted your tax return before 1 April 2026? Then the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) is required to respond before 1 July 2026. If you submit later, it can take months before you receive a notification about a refund.

Why is this deadline so important?

Filing late has immediate financial consequences. The Belastingdienst automatically imposes a default penalty if you still have not filed a tax return after 1 May 2026 and have not applied for an extension.

  • First default: fine from €385
  • Repeated default: fine up to €6,709
  • Intentional non-filing: offence penalty up to 100% of the tax owed

In addition to the fine, you may also have to pay tax interest if you owe tax. In 2026 the tax interest is 6.5% per year and starts from 1 July 2026.

What should you do now?

Depending on your situation there are three options:

Your situationWhat to do
I have time and knowledgeFile the tax return yourself via Mijn Belastingdienst with DigiD
I need more timeApply for an extension (free, until 1 September 2026)
I want it taken off my plateLet us handle it – we arrange everything for €59

This is how to apply for an extension: Log in to Mijn Belastingdienst → go to "Personal details" → click on "Apply for extension". You can do this up to and including 1 May 2026. The extension then runs until 1 September 2026.

What if I have missed the deadline?

Did you miss 1 May 2026? Don't wait any longer. The Belastingdienst first sends a reminder and then a warning. If you don't respond, the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) will estimate your income themselves, often to your disadvantage, and you will receive an assessment that is usually higher.

Good news: you can file a tax return up to 5 years back. Filing sooner prevents higher fines and ensures you still receive any refund. We also help with late tax returns and corrections of old years.

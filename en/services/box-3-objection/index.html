Objecting to box 3 (savings tax) in the Zaanstad region

Box 3 is the tax on your assets (savings and investments). For years the Belastingdienst calculated with a fixed, assumed return that was often higher than the real one — especially for people who mainly held savings.

The Supreme Court ruled that anyone whose real return was lower is entitled to redress over 2021 up to and including 2024. If your money was mostly in a savings account, there is a good chance you paid too much.

We assess your situation per tax year free of charge, file a timely pro-forma objection to keep your rights open, and only handle the main objection on a no cure, no pay basis.

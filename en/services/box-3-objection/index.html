Box 3 for 2021–2024: where things stand

Box 3 is the tax on your assets: savings, investments and, for example, a second home. For years the tax authority used an assumed return, often higher than what savers actually received.

For 2021 up to and including 2024 you may now always report your actual return, with the form Opgaaf werkelijk rendement. You do not need to file an objection and you do not need a letter from the tax authority. The tax authority then uses whichever amount is most favourable for you.

You only file an objection against a final assessment, within 6 weeks of the date on that assessment. So a pro forma objection only makes sense while that period is still running.

◆ Is it worth it for you?

When does reporting your actual return pay off?

It depends on what your assets really earned each year. These are the situations where the chance is greatest.

🏦

Mainly savings

Was your money mainly in a savings account? Multiply your savings on 1 January by that year's assumed percentage and compare the result with the interest you received. If the interest was lower, reporting pays off.

📉

Investments with a low return

Did your investments earn little in a year, or fall in value? Then your actual return may be lower than the assumed one. If it was negative, the tax authority sets it at €0.

📅

Per year, 2021 up to and including 2024

You fill in a separate form for each year. We calculate per year whether it yields something. From 2025 there is no separate form: you report the actual return in your tax return.

⏳

Objections only within 6 weeks

Did you just receive a final assessment you disagree with? Then you can object within 6 weeks of the date on the assessment. We check whether that is necessary in your case.

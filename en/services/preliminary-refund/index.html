Applying for a preliminary refund in the Zaanstad region

If you own your home with a mortgage, you pay less tax thanks to the mortgage interest deduction. Normally you only get that benefit back after your annual return, in one go, a year later. It does not have to be that way.

With a preliminary tax refund (voorlopige teruggave), the Belastingdienst pays out your expected refund monthly, in advance. So you keep more each month. You can apply for or change it all year round.

We arrange the application for you, at your home in Zaandam, Krommenie, Wormerveer, Assendelft or elsewhere in the region. We calculate your benefit correctly, including the owner-occupied home value, so you do not risk having to repay afterwards.

◆ Explanation

How does a preliminary tax refund work?

What it is, which deductions count and what it gives you per month.

The Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) calls a preliminary tax refund a preliminary assessment with a negative amount. You then receive part of your expected refund every month, instead of once a year after your tax return. So you get your money twelve times a year, at the moment you also pay the costs.

After the end of the year you file your normal tax return. If your actual deductions are higher than the estimate, you receive the difference. If they are lower, you pay the difference back. That is why we prefer to estimate on the careful side.

Which deductions can you include?

A preliminary refund can include almost all personal deductions and the costs of an owner-occupied home:

  • Mortgage interest and the imputed rental value (eigenwoningforfait)
  • Specific healthcare costs, above the threshold
  • Donations to a charity (ANBI), above the threshold or as a periodic gift
  • Partner alimony that you pay
  • Weekend expenses for a child with a disability
  • Maintenance of children

You fill in an estimate for the whole year. Be careful with it: an estimate that is too high means paying back afterwards. We calculate your amounts in advance.

Example: a mortgage of €340,000

Say your home has a WOZ value of €340,000 and you have a mortgage of €340,000 at 3.7% interest. Your income is in the first tax bracket, so you deduct at 37.56%.

Item Per year Per month
Mortgage interest €12,580 €1,048
Imputed rental value (0.35%) − €1,190 − €99
Deductible balance €11,390 €949
Tax benefit (37.56%) ± €4,278 ± €357

* Indicative example. Your actual refund depends on your interest rate, WOZ value, tax bracket and situation. This example assumes an income in the first tax bracket. In the top bracket the interest is deducted at the capped rate while the imputed rental value is added to your income at the full rate, so the benefit is lower.

When do you receive the money?

After approval, the Belastingdienst pays out monthly, usually around the 20th. Apply in January and the amount is spread over twelve months. Apply in April and you receive the first three months in one payment, followed by the fixed monthly amount.

Changing your preliminary refund

You can adjust a running preliminary assessment at any time. Do so in any case if:

  • your mortgage changes, for example after a new fixed-interest period;
  • your income goes up or down;
  • you have high healthcare costs you want to include;
  • you get divorced and start paying alimony;
  • you move, or buy or sell a home.

If your situation changes and the assessment does not, you pay back afterwards. We check that for you free of charge.

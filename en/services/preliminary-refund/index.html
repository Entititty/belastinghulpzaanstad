Applying for a preliminary refund in the Zaanstad region

If you own your home with a mortgage, you pay less tax thanks to the mortgage interest deduction. Normally you only get that benefit back after your annual return — in one go, a year later. It does not have to be that way.

With a preliminary tax refund (voorlopige teruggave), the Belastingdienst pays out your expected refund monthly, in advance. So you keep more each month. You can apply for or change it all year round.

We arrange the application for you, at your home in Zaandam, Krommenie, Wormerveer, Assendelft or elsewhere in the region. We calculate your benefit correctly — including the owner-occupied home value — so you do not risk having to repay afterwards.

◆ When it is worth it

For whom and when?

A preliminary refund is not only for first-time buyers. When your situation changes, it is smart to have it applied for or updated.

🔑

Just bought a home

First-time buyers often leave money on the table for months. Apply for a preliminary refund right away and receive your mortgage benefit from the first month.

📦

Moved or new mortgage

After moving, refinancing or a new mortgage, your deduction changes. We update your preliminary refund so the amount is correct again.

💼

Income changed

Earned more or less, retired or changed jobs? A changed income changes your refund — have it adjusted to avoid repaying later.

🧮

Not sure it is right

We check your current preliminary assessment for free, so you know you are not receiving too much or too little.

◆ Frequently asked questions

Questions about the preliminary refund

It is a preliminary assessment in which the Belastingdienst pays out your expected refund (such as your mortgage interest deduction) monthly, instead of in one go after your annual return. So you keep more each month.

Yes. You can apply for or change a preliminary refund all year round. If you apply later in the year, the months already missed are usually settled in the next instalments.

That depends on your mortgage interest, income and tax bracket. For many homeowners it is between €100 and €400 per month. We calculate your exact amount before we submit the application.

If you receive too much, the Belastingdienst settles it after your annual return. That is why we calculate realistically and update your refund as soon as your situation changes, so you are not caught out.

The application alone costs €49; the complete package with mortgage calculation is €109 (incl. VAT). We need your mortgage details, property valuation (WOZ) and income information — we tell you exactly what.

Every year, more people get stuck on the Dutch tax return — not because it truly has to be complicated, but because everything is online, the Belastingdienst language is hard to follow, and DigiD is a real barrier for many seniors and expats.

◆ Who it is for

Made for people, not corporations

We focus on private individuals — seniors, first-time home buyers and expats — not on complex company accounts.

👴

Seniors & AOW pensioners

We make sure you use the elderly person's tax credit (ouderenkorting), healthcare cost and gift deductions, and we check your rent and healthcare benefit while we are at it.

🔑

First-time buyers

Bought your first home? You may be entitled to receive your mortgage tax benefit every month. We arrange your return and your preliminary refund together.

🌍

Expats

New to the Dutch system? We explain what the form says and why, in plain English, and file a correct return — including the year you arrived or left (M-form).

📄

Missed a year

Did not file, or missed deductions? We can correct returns up to five years back and still file a late return for you.

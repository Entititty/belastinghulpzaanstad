Help with inheritance and gift tax in the Zaanstad region

Losing someone is hard enough without a tax form on top of it. After a death, an inheritance tax return (aangifte erfbelasting) generally has to be filed within eight months. We keep that deadline and make sure everything is done correctly and on time.

Would you rather give during your lifetime? There is an annual exemption for gifts to children, and under conditions a one-off increased exemption. The exact amounts are set each year, we calculate the current exemptions for your situation so you give in the most tax-efficient way.

We guide you calmly through the whole process, at your home in Zaandam, Krommenie, Wormerveer or elsewhere in the region, and tell you honestly when a civil-law notary is needed.

◆ Frequently asked questions

Questions about inheritance and gift tax

After a death, the inheritance tax return generally has to be filed within eight months. We monitor that deadline and make sure everything is done on time and correctly.

There is an annual exemption for gifts to children, and under conditions a one-off increased exemption. The exact amounts are set each year, we calculate the current exemptions for your situation.

Because every estate and gift is different, we work on quote. You receive a clear, no-obligation price upfront, so you are never caught out.

For the tax return itself a notary is not always needed. For certain matters (such as a will or a paper gift) it is. We tell you honestly when a notary is required and work with one where needed.

For inheritance tax: details of the estate (assets, debts, the WOZ value of a home) and the heirs. For a gift: the amount and your relationship to the recipient. We tell you exactly what we need.

Correcting or filing a late return in the Zaanstad region

Did you forget deductions, or did you miss the filing deadline? That is more common than you think — and in most cases it can still be fixed. The Belastingdienst allows returns to be corrected up to five years back.

We reclaim missed deductions over 2021–2024 and file your late 2025 return. The Belastingdienst first sends a reminder and then a formal notice (aanmaning); a penalty (€469 the first time) only follows if you do not respond. The sooner you file, the smaller the risk.

You pay a fixed €69 per annual return, incl. VAT. We check beforehand for free whether a correction actually pays off — no false promises.

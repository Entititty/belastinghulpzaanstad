◆ About us

A new, local tax office, built honestly

Belastinghulp Zaanstad started in 2026 with one goal: simple, personal help with the Dutch tax return for people in Zaanstad who do not want to fight with DigiD online, and just want someone to sit at the kitchen table with them. We are new. And we are honest about that. We prefer an honest start over a foundation of fake social proof.

Why we started

More and more people get stuck on the Dutch tax return. Not because it really has to be that complicated, but because everything is online, the language used by the Belastingdienst (Dutch tax authority) is hard to follow, and DigiD is a real barrier for many seniors and expats. First-time home buyers often do not know they can receive their tax benefit every month through a preliminary tax refund (preliminary teruggave).

We wanted an alternative: not a big accountancy chain with waiting times and unclear hourly rates, but a small local office in the Zaanstad municipality that comes to your home, calmly walks through everything with you, and tells you the price upfront.

Who comes to your table

Stan van der Hoeven

Founder of Belastinghulp Zaanstad

Stan has been preparing income tax returns for eight years, starting through his work, and has done more than a hundred. He found that he enjoys working out how things fit together: what is allowed and what is not. What he enjoys even more is the contact with people: going through the paperwork together at the table, explaining what happens, and seeing someone relieved once it is sorted. That is why he started Belastinghulp Zaanstad in 2026.

Stan visits you at home, takes his time, and explains in plain words what happens and why. He works mostly with pensioners, people on AOW, and homeowners in Zaandam, Koog aan de Zaan, Wormerveer, Krommenie, Assendelft, Westzaan, Zaandijk, Oostzaan and Wormerland.

He writes and updates the articles on this site himself, with the amounts and thresholds of the current tax year. If something is wrong or missing, tell him.
